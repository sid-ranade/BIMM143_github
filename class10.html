#Q4: Water molecules normally have 3 atoms. Why do we see just one atom per water molecule in this structure?

PDB structures can only resolve the oxygen atom in H2O and so therefore only see one atom in the molecule. Hydrogen atoms scatter Xrays weakly for Xray crystalography techniques.
